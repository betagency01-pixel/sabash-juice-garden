# Sabash Juice Garden — AI image prompts

Use these in an image generator (Midjourney, Gemini/Imagen, DALL·E, Ideogram, Firefly).

generate without text and let the website add the branding.

Brand look: deep red #D7101E as hero colour, black #0A0A0A, warm white #FBF9F6.
Bold, editorial, premium food photography. No cartoon, no clutter.

Save the results as JPG (1600px on the long side) into the `assets/` folder and tell me
the filenames. I'll put them into the site.
